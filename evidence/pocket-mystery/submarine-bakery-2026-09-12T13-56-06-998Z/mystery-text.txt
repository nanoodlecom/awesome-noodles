TITLE
The Lullaby of the Sunken Salon

PLAYER HANDOUT
Long ago, a mer-composer kept a music salon in this glass-walled chamber, where a "tide-lullaby" was played each dusk to coax the seaweed gardens to sleep. The song has gone unsung for a hundred years, and the garden grows restless. You, a tide-restorer, can awaken it — but only if you greet the salon's three wonders in the order the old songbook describes.

Your goal: restore the tide-lullaby by pointing to the three wonders in the correct order.

The three wonders:
- The brick dome structure — lower left on the sandy floor, a small domed shelter with an arched opening.
- The spiral shell display — center, resting on a rectangular pedestal against the back wall.
- The circular porthole — upper right, mounted on the curved tiled wall.

From the recovered songbook:
- Clue of the Opening: "Greet not the striped shell until the humble domed home has heard your first kind word."
- Clue of the Light: "The sea first shows her face through the round brown window; only then may you wander to the little brick house."

Rules:
- Point to each of the three landmarks exactly once.
- Commit to a complete order of all three before the facilitator responds.
- If your order is incorrect, you'll receive gentle feedback and may try again fresh — nothing carries over between attempts.
- Any "magical" results are narrated fiction only; the image never changes.
