**Story beat**  
In Frame 1, the tiny postal robot approaches the sealed circular hatch from an exterior/corridor space, reaching for the latch while holding a domed capsule containing a seedling. In Frame 2, the hatch is open, and the robot crouches before a circular soil tray containing a newly planted seedling. However, rather than fully advancing the camera inside the greenhouse, the shot stays in the same corridor looking at the open hatch, and the robot still holds a second seedling inside its jar, failing to resolve into an empty capsule.
