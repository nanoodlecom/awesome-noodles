Mesh Diet — photo → game-budget mesh (Demand Watch cycle 6, shipped 2026-11-19)

WHAT IT DOES
One photo of one object becomes a mesh an engine will accept. Idol Max Turbo
rebuilds the photo as a textured mesh of exactly 30,000 triangles; Hyper3D Low
Poly (new on NanoGPT 2026-11-18) then retopologizes it down to a face budget
you set — 4,000 in the demo, inside its 200-10,000 range — with UVs and PBR
textures carried over. A Gemini surveyor first writes down the silhouette
features that must survive the cut, and a GLM guide closes with the budget
table and the honest edges. Already have a heavy GLB? Wire it straight into
the Low Poly node's model input and skip the rebuild — retopology is the
point, the rebuild is just the demo's way of guaranteeing a heavy mesh.

THE PROOF IS COUNTABLE (parsed from the actual GLB files, not the marketing)
Probe (2026-11-19): 30,000 triangles in → 3,876 out (target 4,000).
Saved run below: 30,000 in → 3,929 out. Automatic retopology lands NEAR its
target, not on it — the guide says so, and the counts are printed here so
anyone can re-parse the files and check. Silhouette verified by rendering
both meshes from three angles: wings, coiled tail, antlered head and plinth
all read at ~4k triangles; the whiskers and teeth merge, as the survey
predicted.

THE HONEST EDGES (also in the guide)
Automatic retopology is not hand retopology: a character that must deform
at the joints still wants edge loops a person designs. Props, environment
pieces and LODs are the claim. And the diet is in triangles, not megabytes —
the saved diet GLB is 11.8 MB against the heavy mesh's 10.0 MB, because the
transferred textures are as large as the source's; texture resolution is a
separate lever.
