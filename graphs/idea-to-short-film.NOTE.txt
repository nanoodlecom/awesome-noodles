# recipe-note
One sentence becomes a 15-second film that plays as one scene. GLM 5.3 Flash writes a style bible (the hero, the place, one time of day, the light, the lens, the look) and a setup / turn / payoff story where each shot ends on the frame the next one starts from. Four Join nodes paste the style bible word for word in front of the opening-still prompt and each shot prompt. Muse paints the opening still; three Seedance 1.5 Pro image-to-video shots follow, and Video → frames hands each shot's last frame to the next, so cuts continue the action. Combine joins the clips (hard cuts; it has no crossfade) and Soundtrack lays one ElevenLabs music bed over the film. Inspired by ComfyUI-MiniMaxH3-Studio by @aiaicreate (https://x.com/aiaicreate/status/2107279553609830408). Only the idea is used.

Change **Film idea**: one sentence with a hero and a beginning, middle and end. The graph exports **Short film**. The style bible, story, still, clips, last frames and music are intermediates you can see on the canvas.

Defaults are 720p, 5 s, 16:9, Generate Audio off for every shot, and a 15-second instrumental bed. On 8 October 2026 the nodes in the saved film reported $0.55: $0.13 per Seedance shot, $0.15 for the music, $0.01 for the still and about $0.0001 per GLM call. Prices are observations, not caps. Video → frames, Combine and Soundtrack run locally; headless runs need `ffmpeg` on PATH.

The saved run is in the nanoodle gallery (https://nanoodle.com/examples/gallery/#idea-to-short-film). Setup: a small red-sleeved hand sets a yellow paper boat in a rainy gutter. Turn: the boat drifts to an open drain and tips over the edge. Payoff: the hand lifts it out. One look and one place throughout. Flaws: a hand flicks through a corner of shot 2 for about half a second, the music fades about 2 seconds early, and the cuts are hard. Shots 2 and 3 were re-rendered once (see run.json in the gallery).
