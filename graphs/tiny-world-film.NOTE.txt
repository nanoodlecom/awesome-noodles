# recipe-note
Three models make one finished short: Muse generates a miniature world, Wan animates the actual image, and Mirelo generates foley from the actual video. The final stage is a recovered component from the removed `mirelo-video-foley` wrapper, now doing a job inside a complete workflow.

Change **World** and **Motion** together. The graph exports **First frame** and **Film with foley**. **Silent take** is the intermediate animation, visible on the canvas and retained by the evidence runner. Compare both clips: the sound model does not receive the original scene description, and its sounds need to fit the motion that was actually generated.

Defaults request 81 frames at 16 fps and 480p (about five seconds). On the 12 September 2026 catalog, the image is $0.01, animation $0.20, and foley approximately $0.05. These are estimates, not caps; the provider may round duration. Higher settings and reruns cost extra. No music, speech or exact synchronization is promised.

The graph uses the existing image, image-to-video, video-edit and resize nodes. Node >=20 and `ffmpeg` on PATH cover headless media conversion; the Python executor needs `ffmpeg` for local media. The evidence runner saves intermediate video as well as final outputs. Models are pinned for reproducibility; inspect the live catalogs before refreshing them.

Default and alternate briefs with acceptance criteria: `fixtures/tiny-world-film.cases.json`. The [selected runs and review notes](../evidence/README.md#tiny-world-film) include the actual stills, silent takes and sounded films. Both final films are five-second H.264 at 832×480 with AAC. Two model-assisted audio reviews describe fitting sounds without speech or music; this is not human listening sign-off or a precise synchronization test. The moth's orange underside changes the intended material. The final generation runs each reported about $0.18176 on this account, before the separate audio review; future charges may differ from these observed costs and the catalog estimates above.
