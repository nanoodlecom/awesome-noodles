# recipe-note
Four separate references go into one picture. Each reference is its own node: who (a character), what they wear (an outfit), what they hold (a prop) and where they stand (a place). FLUX.3 Klein Edit gets them in port order (image, image2, image3, image4), and the mix instruction names each one by number: "Image 1 is the character… Image 2 is the outfit…". Each part of the picture comes from its own source, not from one long prompt.

Change any of the four reference prompts, or replace a reference Image node with a 📷 Image input and drop in your own photo (a product, a mascot, a jacket, a room). Keep the wiring order and update the matching "Image N is…" line in **Mix instruction (names each image)**. Klein takes up to 10 references. The graph exports the **Mix · FLUX.3 Klein Edit** image.

Defaults are four Muse 1:1 stills and one Klein Edit at 1024×1024. On 16 October 2026 the saved run reported $0.053: 4 × $0.011 for Muse and $0.009 for Klein. With your own uploads in place of the Muse stills, a run is about $0.01. Prices are observations, not caps.

The saved run is in the nanoodle gallery (https://nanoodle.com/examples/gallery/#mix-the-references): a paper-craft fox in the yellow knitted raincoat (hood up, three wooden toggles) holds the one red paper lantern on a cardboard pier at dusk, with the striped lighthouse behind it. Flaws: the two paper boats from the place reference mostly drop out, the fox's ears poke through the hood, and the coat comes out longer than the flat-lay reference. Without "exactly one lantern" in the instruction, an earlier run gave the fox two lanterns. Inspired by the multi-reference workflows ComfyUI showed for Krea 3 and FLUX.3 Klein (https://x.com/ComfyUI/status/2110840667337642330); no media from that post is used.
