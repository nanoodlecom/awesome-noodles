# recipe-note
Every other spoken flow in this catalog puts its payload in the words. This one forbids the words to carry anything at all. Give it a setting (the default is a community noticeboard bulletin) and a GLM director writes a FEELING CARD: one spec chosen from a menu of four (GOOD NEWS joyful, SAD NEWS heartbroken, BAD NEWS furious, SCARY NEWS terrified), each an EMOTION, the ElevenLabs V3 audio TAG that performs it ([excited], [sad], [angry], [frightened]), a DELIVERY line, and one emotionally NEUTRAL bulletin sentence - "The library will reopen on Monday morning at nine o'clock" contains no word that names or suggests joy, and that is the point. A second GLM, the actor, writes the SPEECH SCRIPT: the tag, then the sentence word for word. ElevenLabs V3 (voice Sarah) performs it. The take is then split into the two channels a voice carries. Whisper transcribes the WORDS - a channel that provably cannot carry the payload, since the sentence is identical whatever the feeling - and a blind Qwen Omni listener, who hears the audio and nothing else, names the EMOTION it actually heard, with its energy and pace. A GLM auditor scores EMOTION (the listener's word against the card: MATCH / NEAR / MISS), WORDS KEPT (the transcript against the sentence, word for word), and TAG SPOKEN (the named failure: the direction tag read aloud as a word - "excited" spoken at the front of the bulletin instead of performed). A ⚖️ Decide judge (PPLX Decider) headlines round 1. That verdict is not the deliverable: a GLM editor repairs ONLY the performance - a stronger or better-fitting tag, one tag moved mid-sentence before the phrase that should land hardest, delivery punctuation - under a hard rule that the sentence's words never change, because the words are the controlled variable; on a perfect audit it must demand the round-1 script verbatim. The same voice re-reads the corrected script, and a blind re-listen plus the identical re-audit score it against the same card. The corrected reading is the flow's final output.

Why the loop matters: the catalog's two nearest neighbours both audit sound, but neither can state this flow's question. Rhyme check's payload - end words, meter - is a property of the TEXT and survives transcription, so its ear can be Whisper; score the still's payload is a music cue's mood, composed from a picture and heard as axes. Here a perfect transcript proves nothing: the words are the control group, held word-for-word identical across both rounds so that the ONLY thing the editor is allowed to move is the performance, and the only instrument that can grade the result is a listener that hears pitch, pace and energy. That is also why TAG SPOKEN is scored as its own failure - a speech model that reads its stage direction aloud has leaked the payload into the words channel, where any transcript would happily certify it. Prosody is the channel every voice product actually ships on - the same bulletin read flat and read joyful are different announcements - and no foreground audit of the text will ever report which one went out.

Honest limits, in the open: the emotion read is a single blind listener choosing from six words, and emotion-naming is judgement, not measurement - a warm calm can sit between joyful and calm, and the NEAR band absorbs only part of that looseness; the auditor is told joyful-heard-as-calm is a payload miss, not a near. The delivery channel itself is one vendor feature: ElevenLabs V3 audio tags are prompt-shaped, not guaranteed - a tag can be underplayed, overplayed, or spoken, and the flow can only repair within the same tag vocabulary plus punctuation, never with a different voice or a slower base rate. And the controlled variable is the sentence, not the speaker: both rounds use the same voice (Sarah), so the audit measures this voice's range, not voices in general.

Live proof (2026-11-21, flow-hunt 18:30 session): ONE live run, SUCCESS end to end - all 23 nodes done, no errors, no staging spend - $0.0257 balance-verified ($24.7165 → $24.6908; metered $0.0237 - two ElevenLabs V3 takes $0.01207 each, two Qwen listener calls $0.00009 total, GLM $0.00; Whisper and Decide unpriced in the report, cost_exact=false; the balance at session start was $25.4932, ~$0.78 of drops outside this ledger crossed the shared account before the run). The director carded GOOD NEWS exactly: EMOTION joyful, TAG [excited], SENTENCE "The library will reopen on Monday morning at nine o'clock." The words channel was perfect twice: both transcripts kept 10 of 10 words in order ("nine" heard as "9" - value-preserving), and TAG SPOKEN was NO in both rounds - the tag was never read aloud. The voice channel failed twice, identically: the blind listener heard EMOTION calm, ENERGY low, PACE moderate, VOICE "steady and neutral" in round 1, and the auditor scored EMOTION: MISS with Decide v1.2 at 'flat take'. The gated editor repaired only the performance, as designed - same tag, an ellipsis before the final phrase, the full stop lifted to an exclamation mark, not one word changed - and the corrected take is measurably different audio (4.83s against round 1's 3.55s, the pause is real) yet the blind re-listen returned the same report word for word: calm, low, steady and neutral, EMOTION: MISS again. So the flow's first outing proves the audit's teeth and prices its repair channel in one run: through this executor and voice, a leading [excited] tag plus delivery punctuation cannot make a neutral bulletin sound joyful to a blind ear - the payload channel the flow exists to measure is exactly the channel that failed, and both the failure and the failed repair are in the open rather than smoothed over.
