# recipe-note
A Necker cube is one set of twelve lines that supports two opposite solids: read the bottom-left square as the front face and the cube recedes up-right; read the top-right square as the front face and the same strokes recede down-left instead. Neither parse is wrong, and nothing in the pixels may favour either - the moment a face is shaded, filled, drawn heavier or given broken hidden edges, the drawing stops being bistable and becomes an ordinary picture of one cube. Vision-language models are known to collapse exactly this ambiguity: on bistable figures they report one dominant interpretation, driven by prior rather than by the strokes, and the rival parse goes unmentioned. That makes certified ambiguity a clean audit axis, and one no single read can certify: a neutral reader shows only which parse dominates, a primed reader shows only that a suggested parse can be held, and either can be fooled - a cued drawing fools the neutral reader into confidence, and a suggestible primed reader will 'hold' a parse the pixels fight. This flow certifies the conjunction by construction. Give it a plain backdrop and a GLM director picks one cube from a menu printed in its own prompt (EMBER CUBE in black ink, INDIGO CUBE in deep indigo, FOREST CUBE in dark forest green - the geometry is fixed across the menu so the two parses can be named in advance) and copies it onto a BISTABLE CARD: the geometry (two equal squares, one bottom-left and one offset up-right, joined by four straight connectors - twelve edges, eight corners), PARSE A (front face BOTTOM-LEFT), PARSE B (front face TOP-RIGHT), the ink, and the cue ban. A second GLM turns the card into a painter prompt that demands a pure textbook wireframe and forbids every disambiguating cue by name. Muse draws it.

Three blind Gemini readers then work the same drawing, none having seen the card. The NEUTRAL reader, given no interpretation, reports which face it sees in front and inventories any depth cue the lines carry (shaded or filled faces, broken edges, unequal line weight, extra marks). The PRIMED-A reader is asked to hold parse A - bottom-left square as front face - and honestly reports the face it actually sees in front and whether that parse coheres. The PRIMED-B reader does the same for parse B. A GLM auditor scores the three reads against the card: a primed parse is RECOVERED only when that reader's front face matches its prime and it calls the parse coherent; the LEAK line copies the neutral reader's cue inventory. The verdict follows a stated precedence: FLAT when no parse is recoverable at all; FORCED when a cue leaked, whatever the primed readers managed - the pixels disambiguated, so the ambiguity was never the drawing's; BISTABLE only when both primed parses are recovered AND the leak is NONE; ONE-SIDED when exactly one parse recovers on clean pixels. A Decide judge (PPLX Decider) headlines round 1 as a side output - **truly bistable**, **forced by a cue**, **one parse only**, **flat wireframe**, or **not a cube** - but the deliverable is the corrected figure.

That verdict is not where this flow stops - the loop closes, per the standing rule that a created asset ends in the corrected asset, not in text. A GLM editor reads the card and the audit and writes ONE concrete repair instruction, and it is gated in content, the rule this catalog learned from Molecule Check: on a BISTABLE audit the editor must demand a verbatim unchanged copy. On any other verdict the editor's hands are tied in the opposite direction from an ordinary repair: it may only REMOVE - unfill and unshade a face, restore a broken edge to one continuous line, equalise line weight, complete a missing edge - and it is forbidden ever to add a cue, because 'fixing' a one-sided figure by shading the losing face would not repair the ambiguity, it would destroy it and call the destruction a pass. Muse Edit repairs, all three blind readers re-read the corrected figure, and the same auditor re-scores it against the same card. The flow's final output is the corrected figure itself.

The honest limits are stated in the open. Coherence is a reader's report, not a measurement: a primed reader can claim a parse coheres out of compliance with its prime, which is why the neutral reader's unprimed parse and cue inventory are scored alongside - a FORCED verdict can be reached on pixels alone, but a BISTABLE verdict always rests partly on two primed self-reports, and the NOTE records them as such. The neutral reader's cue inventory is the softest pixel evidence in the audit: light shading at painterly resolution is a judgement call, and a missed cue manufactures a false BISTABLE just as a hallucinated cue manufactures a false FORCED. The menu's variety is presentational, not geometric - one Necker orientation, three inks - because the primed asks must name the parses in advance; a mirrored cube would need its own card family. One run proves the chain - director, brief, paint, triple blind reads, conjunction audit, gated repair, re-audit - end to end on the cube the director happened to pick.
