## Notes

Two loops are built in parallel and joined. FLUX.3 Klein paints a rainy bookshop window, Pixelcut Looping Video animates that still into a 5 s clip with subtle motion, and ElevenLabs Sound Effects V3 generates 5 s of rain on glass with its Seamless loop switch on (`{"loop": true}` in Advanced params). The Soundtrack node, with loop on, marries them: an ambient loop where the sound wraps click-free and the picture almost does.

Change **Scene** and **Sound** together; the graph exports the single Soundtrack output. The two branches are independent until the join, so either side can be re-run or swapped alone — a different still model, a different ambience, the same shape.

Defaults are a 1024×1024 still, a 480p 5 s picture loop and 5 s of sound. Observed on the first live run (7 November 2026): still $0.009, picture loop $0.40 flat, sound about $0.01 after reconciliation — the graph totalled about $0.42. These are observations, not caps. Pixelcut charges the same $0.08/s at every resolution, so 480p saves nothing; 1080p costs the same and looks better.

The seams were measured, and they split. The sound is seamless at sample level: the wrap step (0.00069) matches the median sample step (0.00076), and spectral flux at the wraps sits at the 84th and 91st percentile of the clip, under the ordinary rain texture. The picture is not seamless: the last-to-first frame jump is 2.60 mean absolute difference against a median frame step of 0.43, about 6× normal — the steam plume and rain streaks visibly reset at the loop point. Subtle motion mode does not land back on its input picture.

Two more honest flaws. The lengths disagree: the sound is exactly 5.000 s, the picture 5.167 s (Pixelcut returns 124 frames for "5 s"), so the soundtracked file restarts the sound 0.17 s past its seamless point on every wrap; SFX durations step by 0.1 s, so 5.2 s would land within 33 ms, and an exact fix needs a trim or retime node, which does not exist yet. And the rain is quiet — RMS −44 dBFS — with no gain node to lift it inside the graph.

Size trap: on this route FLUX.3 Klein accepts only 768×768, 1024×1024, 1536×1536, 2048×2048 and 4096×4096. A 16:9 request (1344×768) was refused with a free 400, and the size list the executor's inspect shows disagrees with the API's own list. The graph stays on 1024×1024, which is known to work.

First run and full measurements: the run was made with nanoodle-js on 2026-11-07; the outputs (still, silent loop, rain loop, soundtracked loop, seam card and metrics) are hosted at https://media.nanoodle.com/subnano/2026-11-07-rain-window-loop/ and written up at https://subnano.me/@nano_maxi/rainy-window-loop-for-047-the-rain-sound-wraps-cleanly-but-the-picture-still-pops. Models are pinned for reproducibility; inspect the live catalogs before refreshing them.
