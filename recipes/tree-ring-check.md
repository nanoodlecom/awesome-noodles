## Notes

A tree trunk is a diary that writes itself radially. Each year adds exactly one growth ring around everything that came before: a wet, kind year lays down a broad band of pale wood, a drought year a thin dark-edged one, so a sawn cross-section holds the tree's whole weather history in centre-out order - ring 1, around the pith, is the first year of its life, and the ring just inside the bark is the year it was felled. Read in order, the widths spell the years; read carelessly, they are just decoration, and decoration is what image models paint - even, wallpaper rings, sometimes splitting or doubling, that spell no history at all. This flow makes the diary a planned, measurable quantity - and then it does something about a false entry. Give it a plain cross-section setting and a GLM director picks one tree from a menu printed in its own prompt (RIVER OAK, HILL PINE, ORCHARD ASH) and copies it onto a RING CARD: exactly nine rings, the width class of each counted from the centre out (WIDE, MEDIUM or NARROW), every menu tree holding the same three-wide, three-medium, three-narrow multiset shuffled into a different order, so no habit - alternation, a staircase, a repeated rhythm - can fake the card. A second GLM turns the card into one painter prompt demanding a straight-on cross-section: a small dark pith at the centre, nine complete closed rings at the carded widths, one unbroken bark rim. Muse paints it. A blind Gemini reader, who never saw the card, counts the rings and classes each one's width from the pith outward. A GLM auditor scores COUNT, WIDTHS position by position, and BARK: GROWTH TRUE only when the count is nine, all nine widths match and the rim is intact; COUNT DRIFTED when the painted tree lived a different number of years; PATTERN BROKEN when nine rings carry a wrong width. A Decide judge (PPLX Decider) headlines round 1 as a side output.

That verdict is not where this flow stops - the loop closes, per the standing rule that a created asset ends in the corrected asset, not in text. A GLM editor reads the card, the read and the audit and writes ONE concrete edit instruction: the full target end state (nine rings, the card's centre-out width sequence, the unbroken rim) plus the audit's specific failures as extra demands, each failing ring located by its centre-out number and its neighbours - widen the fourth ring out from the pith, narrow the seventh. The editor is gated in content, the rule this catalog learned from Molecule Check: on a GROWTH TRUE audit it must demand a verbatim unchanged copy and forbid any ring being redrawn. Muse Edit applies the notes. The corrected cross-section is the flow's deliverable. A second blind reader re-enumerates it centre-out, and the same auditor scores round 2 against the same card, so the fix is itself audited and the reader can see exactly what the edit bought - or cost.

Why this axis is new: the catalog's nearest neighbours are bar-chart-audit and capillary-check, and the differences are mechanical. A bar chart's values stand as separate objects on a shared baseline, each readable without reference to the others; capillary's columns stand in separate tubes, each derivable from its own tube's bore. A ring sequence is nested and ordered by containment: there is no baseline and no per-object label, the sequence exists only as centre-out position, and a single merged or split ring shifts every later position's meaning - one local error corrupts the whole suffix of the diary, a failure shape neither neighbour can produce. The audit's unit is also new: not a height, a count of objects, or a law between two quantities, but the match of a nine-year width history, where the same multiset in a different order is a different tree's life and scores as broken. And the count itself is evidence here, not bookkeeping: nine rings is nine years, so a painter who adds a decorative ring has not embellished the picture, it has falsified the tree's age.

Honest limits, in the open: width classes are judged relative to the other rings in the same picture (broad band about three times the thinnest, medium about twice), so a painter who scales every ring evenly keeps the pattern while changing the tree's absolute growth - the flow certifies the history's shape, not its millimetres. A ring whose thickness sits between classes is a genuine naming border for the reader; the read records the call it made, and both blind reads are independent, which is the only guard. The auditor compares sequences position by position, not by similarity: a diary shifted by one ring scores badly even though it holds the same years, which is the point - the card is exact, and centre-out position is the meaning. The fix round has its own limit: an edit model asked to widen one ring can nudge its neighbours - that is exactly what round 2's WIDTHS line is for, and both audits are printed so the reader can see the trade. One repair round is unrolled, not a loop - if round 2 still violates, the flow shows the violation rather than spending again.
