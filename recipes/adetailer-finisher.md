## Notes

The ComfyUI ADetailer pattern, rebuilt from Nanoodle parts. In ComfyUI, ADetailer detects a face or hand, crops it, re-renders the crop at full resolution, and pastes it back. Nanoodle has no detector, crop, or paste-back node, so this graph moves each job to the nearest node that can do it: a Gemini vision node is the detector (it lists concrete defects, with locations), a GLM node turns the list into one surgical edit instruction, and two different edit models take the refine passes — Muse Edit first, then Nano Banana Edit, which also gets the untouched original as a second image purely as an identity reference. A second vision look writes the remaining-defect list that briefs pass 2, so the second pass only chases what the first one missed.

The piece that makes whole-frame passes safe is the ⚖️ Decide pick at the end. It compares the original and both passes and keeps the most faithful, artifact-free one — a fix that drifts the person's identity or melts a background loses to the untouched upload. The winner then goes through Topaz Image Upscale, the detail-then-upscale finish ComfyUI chains usually end with. The graph exports the **Finished portrait — Topaz Image Upscale** image.

Honest limits, in the open: the passes are whole-frame, because there is no crop or mask step to localise them — localisation lives in the instruction, not in a bounding box. Graphs are DAGs, so pass 2 always runs and always bills; when the re-check says CLEAN its brief is a near-no-op polish, and the Decide pick is what protects you from it. The pick judges candidates the decision route shrinks (512 px long edge in the headless executor's fallback limits), which is plenty for fused fingers and warped eyes and weaker for subtle skin artifacts. And an edit model can always refuse to stay surgical — that is exactly the failure the pick exists to catch, not a failure the graph prevents.

Expected cost per run, from the 2026-09-12 catalog snapshot in evidence/catalogs-2026-09-12.json and the gallery's observed runs: Muse Edit $0.01, Nano Banana Edit $0.039, two Gemini vision looks roughly $0.001–0.004 total, the two GLM briefs effectively $0.00–0.001. The Decide pick and the Topaz upscale are not priced in that snapshot, so the honest total is "about $0.05 of known-priced calls, plus those two — expect roughly $0.06–$0.10 all in." Prices are observations, not caps. Repair history: the original finisher, clarity-upscaler, is not a live NanoGPT image model — a live run on 2026-11-07 spent $0.0594 through detect, briefs, both edit passes and the Decide pick, then failed at the finisher with 400 invalid_model. The finisher was swapped to topaz-image-upscale (editor-sighted 2026-11-02) and re-proven live on 2026-11-07: the full chain ran end to end for $0.1416 balance-verified (Topaz finisher $0.08 of it), the detector and re-check both returned CLEAN so both briefs were the designed no-op polish, the Decide pick chose pass 2 (image 3), and the finished portrait came back at 1536x2688 from the 1152x2048 input. Run report: flow-hunt/runs/2026-11-07-adetailer-finisher/report.json in the nanoodle-dev workspace.

Change the **Portrait** input to any photo or generated still with a face or hands in it, and narrow **Watch for** if you only care about one thing ("hands only", "the lettering on the sign"). Swap either edit model in the ⚙️ settings if you prefer a different refiner; keep one of them mask-free — this graph never sends a mask.
