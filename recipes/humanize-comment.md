## Notes

Rewrites a short draft comment so it reads like a person typed it, without changing what it says. This is Nano Maxi's humanize-comment flow v2.7. Five writers rewrite the same draft in parallel, each with its own voice line on a shared base prompt: Qwen3.8-27B Novelist (friend), DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 (forum), TheDrummer Cydonia-24B v4.3 (peer; on mcp.nanoodle.com it falls back to Gemma-4-12B Starry Ocean), Qwen3.8-27B antislop (plain) and Inception Mercury 3 (quick; added 20 October 2026 at Mikkel's request), all reasoningEffort none and maxTokens 600. Each rewrite is joined under a fixed label ([A]..[E]) next to the [ORIGINAL] draft, the post context and the Guidance; the labels are part of the judge joins' separators, so they always stay with their text. A GLM-5.3 decider first checks each rewrite for faithfulness (who said or did what, no new facts, numbers, qualifiers or names, no garbled names, main question and disclosure kept), treats any added explanation, generalization or new claim as unfaithful (when unsure, unfaithful), disqualifies only for unfaithfulness, banned or stock AI phrases, three-part lists, hype, broken grammar or near-verbatim copying, ranks the rest (other style issues such as a dash, a dropped welcome or stiffer wording only lower the rank; direct address to the person and normal sentence casing rank higher, light slang at most once) and picks the most human one that is not disqualified, or ORIGINAL if every rewrite is disqualified. It never picks a near-verbatim copy of ORIGINAL.

Inputs: **Draft** (required; the comment to rewrite, any language, at most 1500 characters), **Context** (required; the post or thread, plain text, at most 3500 characters) and optional **Guidance** (what the comment should do, at most 300 characters; default "a friendly, specific reply"). On mcp.nanoodle.com (nanoodle-mcp) these caps are enforced: a longer input is refused with a clear error before any quote or payment. Characters are Unicode code points. The graph exports the decider's text: a single JSON object and nothing else: {"winner": "ORIGINAL|A|B|C|D|E", "text": "...", "why": "...", "ranked": [{"writer", "faithful", "disqualified", "reason"}, ...]}, where text is the winning rewrite (the draft for ORIGINAL). ranked always has 5 entries, not-disqualified first and best first. Since v2.7 the decider no longer copies the candidates' texts or model names (that let its maxTokens drop to 1600): run through the graph alone (the editor, nanoodle-js or nanoodle-py), ranked carries writer letters, flags and reasons only, and the one text in the output is the winner's, copied once by the decider. The candidates' own texts are still in the run's per-node outputs (the writer nodes w-a to w-e). On mcp.nanoodle.com every ranked entry also gets its model and exact text (below). faithful is about facts only, disqualified is true only for the reasons above or a missing writer, and only an entry with disqualified:false can win. A skipped writer is still listed, as {writer, faithful:false, disqualified:true, reason:"missing"} with an empty text. Run through the graph alone, the winner's text is copied by the decider model, so a rare small wording change is possible (seen in testing: "gets lost" became "is lost", and a 458-character rewrite came back 2 characters shorter). On mcp.nanoodle.com, nanoodle-mcp post-processes the decider's JSON. It never passes the decider's texts or model names through: each ranked entry's model comes from a fixed A..E writer-to-model record taken from the graph's join labels and writer nodes (for C, the model that actually answered: Cydonia, or Starry Ocean when the fallback ran), and each entry's text, and the top-level text, comes from the writers' own outputs by writer id after the dash fix below (exactly the text the decider read), so credit can't shift when a writer is skipped and texts are byte-exact. ranked always lists A to E exactly once, eligible entries first in the decider's order. A writer with no output is listed as {faithful:false, disqualified:true, reason:"missing"} with an empty text. An entry the decider marks faithful:false (or anything but true) is forced to disqualified:true, so it can never win. A writer the decider left out is added as disqualified. The winner is the decider's pick if that is an entry that is faithful, not disqualified and has text. Otherwise, including when the decider picked ORIGINAL, it is the first such entry in ranked order, and why says the pick was replaced (for ORIGINAL: "The decider chose the original; overridden to the first eligible rewrite"). A rewrite has to survive: ORIGINAL, with the draft as text, is returned only when every rewrite is ineligible. If the decider's reply is not valid JSON or has no ranked list, none of it is passed through: the result is winner ORIGINAL with the draft as text, all five writers listed with their real texts as disqualified ("not judged: decider reply unusable", or "missing"), and a note leading the tool result says so.

Writer calls on mcp.nanoodle.com (v2.7; nanoodle-mcp hands the run a fetch wrapper, so the engine still makes one call per writer node and the graph is unchanged by it): (1) Fallback: if Cydonia (writer C) answers 429 or 5xx, the request errors, or it has not answered within 30 s, the same request is sent once to gemma-4-12b-it-starry-ocean, which is then credited for C, and a note says so. An x402 402 is passed through, never treated as a failure. (2) Dash fix: in every writer output, a spaced em dash, en dash or hyphen becomes ", " and any other em or en dash becomes ", ", before the decider reads it. (3) Near-copy re-ask: if more than 60% of the rewrite's word trigrams also occur in the draft, the same model is asked once more (its messages, its own answer, then "Your last attempt copied the draft. Rewrite it in your own words ..."), and the second answer replaces the first; at most 3 writers are re-asked per run (first come, first served; a later near-copy goes to the decider as is, with a note), no re-ask starts after 35 s, and a re-ask that has not answered within 55 s of the writer call starting is abandoned and the first rewrite is kept, so the writer's 60 s timeout never loses a usable answer; both calls are billed and counted in the run's cost. Payment paths (v2.7 round 3): on mcp.nanoodle.com the dash fix, re-ask and fallback cover both ways nanoodle-mcp can pay NanoGPT. With an API key every writer call is a plain chat request. Keyless (x402 wallet mode), a writer's 402 invoice is settled by nanoodle-mcp's humanize wrapper itself rather than by the nanoodle client's complete-endpoint path, so the paid result is post-processed like any other. Each attempt (first try, fallback, re-ask) gets its own invoice, is paid at most once, and is only ever replayed with the exact request that invoice was issued for. Once a payment for Cydonia's invoice has started there is no fallback to Starry Ocean, so one writer slot is never paid twice; a wallet error only allows the fallback when it proves nothing was sent. A payment that was started but not confirmed is reported in the run's notes with its invoice id for reconciling, and its amount is left out of the cost, which is then labelled a lower bound. A re-ask's payment wait is bounded by the same 55 s deadline, so the first rewrite is kept. With an API key, a call that fails after it may have been sent (a connection reset, say) also makes the total a lower bound; only a failure provably before sending (DNS, connection refused) counts as free. If a writer is cancelled mid-call, its known payments stay in the total and any unconfirmed one stays in the reconcile notes; a payment that settles after the writer stopped waiting is recorded once and logged for the operator. If the fallback fails too, the run's note says so instead of crediting it. The cost of every attempt, including failed fallbacks, paid calls that failed and abandoned re-asks, is added to the run's total that the hosted refund and payout math uses (error answers that carry a price are counted too; when some call's price is unknown the total is a lower bound, and both the cost line and the hosted receipt say so: "settled at estimated cost $X (a lower bound…)" instead of "actual cost"). Each fallback and re-ask is listed in a note at the top of the tool result. Run through the graph alone (the editor, nanoodle-js or nanoodle-py) there is no fallback (a failing C is just skipped), no dash fix and no re-ask; the decider still ranks dashes down and disqualifies near-copies.

The prompts and labels are locked: the five writer nodes and the decider carry fields.locked, so their system prompts (the spec's v2.5 writer prompts and decider rubric, with the v2.7 changes below) are not tool inputs and every run uses the stored values. The tool schema is just Draft, Context and Guidance. The block labels "[ORIGINAL]" and "[A] <model>" to "[E] <model>" (each writer's letter and model name, for example "[E] inception/mercury-3") are fixed in the judge joins' separators rather than separate inputs. Changes from the spec's v2.5 decider prompt, all approved by Nano Maxi: the v2.3 line "A missing [X] block means that writer failed.", a missing or empty writer always listed in ranked, a stricter faithfulness rule (no added explanation, generalization or new claim), a role rule (changing what someone is or did, such as roles or who funded, bought or said something, is unfaithful even by paraphrase), per-entry faithful/disqualified/reason, a single-JSON-object output with text inside it, a near-copy rule (a rewrite that keeps most of the draft's sentences or word order is disqualified; prefer the most freshly-phrased faithful rewrite), and model and text in every ranked entry. v2.7 changes, requested by Nano Maxi: Writer A's voice is now sentence case with normal punctuation, direct address (you/your) and at most one light slang word (it used to say "lowercase-ish is fine"); Writer D may copy no more than five words in a row from the draft; Writer C runs TheDrummer/Cydonia-24B-v4.3 ($0.12 in / $0.15 out per M tokens on NanoGPT, 20 October 2026) instead of Starry Ocean; the decider also reads the Guidance (the judge's first join now takes Context + Guidance), disqualifies only for the Step 2 reasons above, and prefers direct address and normal casing (the v2.5 rule against picking a rewrite that drops the direct address is now a ranking preference). Every writer prompt also asks to skip canned praise but keep real warmth toward the person (talk to them, react to their point, keep a greeting or thanks if the draft has one). The decider's maxTokens is 1600 (4300 on the live v2.6 graph). It no longer copies the five rewrites, only ids, flags, reasons, a one-line why and the winner's text, which fits with room to spare.
